# Hunter (working title): pitch

You're half demon, and you hunt demons the way a serial killer hunts people: stalk them, kill them, strip the scene, mount the trophy in your lair. Every boss you eat moves into your head, gives you its moves, and won't shut up about it. Meanwhile the lawyer who owns your soul says every kill pays down your debt. It doesn't.

Platform: Android phone first, portrait, top-down action RPG.

## Pillars
1. Kills are personal: stalk, ambush, finish, take a trophy. How you kill decides what you get.
2. Voices are your moveset: eaten bosses give you their specials and their opinions.
3. Power costs humanity: the more you take, the less human you, your lair and your ending become.

## Not in this game
- Combos outside combat (exploring stays simple).
- Combo meter feeding corruption (cut Nov 20).
- First-person view (that was a different idea).
