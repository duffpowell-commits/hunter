# Style guide (NOT LOCKED)

Duff asked to see styles 1, 2 and 5 on the same character (Vesper). Comparison: docs/art/explore-20261120/sheet-vesper-styles.png (left to right: pixel, ink x2, puppet x2).

## Candidates
1. Ink and blood (Claude's pick): thick black comic linework, heavy solid-black shadows, flat colors, one accent color per realm (Pews gold, Casino red, Quarry ember orange, Ballroom violet, Nursery sick yellow, Price & Partners cold white). Cutout rigs for animation.
2. Grimy pixel art: 64 px sprites, neon-and-rust palette, chunky hit sparks. Generators fake pixel art, so each sprite needs Foxboard Pixelate + cleanup.
5. Puppet cutout: paper-doll demons on visible brass pins with strings (Price pulling the strings). Cheapest to animate, less punchy.

Rejected for this game: HD-2D (Lethemoor and Chaos already use it). Also floated: painted HD (the Canva hero look; hard to keep on-model across combo frames), PS1 low-poly horror (big 3D workload).

## Rules that hold whatever style wins
- Top-down, high three-quarter angle.
- Sprites on a solid key background (green #00FF00; switch to magenta if a subject wears green).
- No named IP in prompts.
